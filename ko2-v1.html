K.O. II — browser recreation

Inspired by Teenage Engineering EP-133 K.O. II. Synthesised default kit on group A — press pads or keys to play.

Modes

  • MAIN — play & sequence. SOUND — edit pad params. TEMPO — BPM / metronome.
  • SAMPLE — hold a pad to record from mic. SHIFT+SAMPLE — chop selected sample across pads.
  • KEYS — play selected pad chromatically across 12 pads.
  • FX — choose effect (−/+), send with fader. Hold FX + pads = punch-in FX.
  • FADER — hold + pad to assign (LEVEL/PITCH/LPF/…). Move fader to perform.
  • TIMING — toggle quantize. Knob Y = swing. SHIFT+TEMPO = loop length.

Sequencer

  • RECORD then PLAY — record with count-in.
  • While stopped: −/+ move step; hold RECORD + pad = place note.
  • Hold ERASE + pad = erase that pad’s notes.
  • Groups A–D each have their own pattern. SHIFT+MAIN commits a scene.

Computer keyboard

  • Pads: num row / U I O J K L M , . / numpad
  • Groups: A B C D · Play: Space · Record: R
  • Modes: Q Sound · W Main · E Tempo · S Sample · F FX · Z Keys · T Timing · X Erase
  • −/+: [ ] · Help: H or ?

Sampling & files

  • Drag-drop audio onto a pad, or Load audio.
  • Save/Load uses localStorage. Export/Import JSON embeds samples as base64.
  • Bounce WAV renders current patterns for one loop.
